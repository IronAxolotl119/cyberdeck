# PULSE's lines for the 16x2 LCD, shown between notifications.
#
# [mood] starts a group; the lines under it show while PULSE has that mood.
# [any] lines show for every mood. Moods: happy, content, excited, hungry,
# tired, sad, sleeping (joyful/playful count as happy, grumpy/angry/bored as sad).
#
# Fill-ins:  {name} {level} {mood} {species} {stage} {hunger} {energy} {happiness}
# Symbols:   {heart} {face} {wifi} {bell} {bolt} {diamond} {note} {food}
#
# 16 characters fit on one line, 32 over both (word-wrapped). Longer lines
# scroll like a ticker. Emoji and accents are swapped for LCD-safe ones.
# After editing, type `lcd reload` in the console.
